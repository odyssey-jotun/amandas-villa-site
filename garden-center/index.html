Most homes have hardly anything growing in them

Americans spend about 87 percent of their time indoors. When the rooms, the porch and the yard have nothing green in them:

  • Plants keep dying

    The fern turns yellow, the shrub by the mailbox drops its leaves, and nobody can say why. So the porch and the windowsills stay bare.

  • People stop trying

    After a few dead plants, most people decide they just can't keep anything alive.

  • Every home deserves something green growing in it

    People who spend two hours a week around nature are measurably more satisfied with their lives. That shouldn't depend on having a green thumb.

Garden center and plant nursery in White Hall, near Pine Bluff

Most families in Jefferson County spend their days indoors and want a home that feels alive, but it's hard to know which plants will live where. Amanda's Villa runs a full garden center and plant nursery on Dollarway Road in White Hall, a short drive from Pine Bluff, and every sale starts with a question about the spot.

The lot carries plants, shrubs and trees, houseplants and hanging baskets, mulch, soil and landscaping stone, pottery and garden decor, and Arkansas produce. Bring a photo of the space, choose plants picked for its light, and enjoy a home with something green in it.

Garden center questions people ask on the lot

Can you help me choose plants?

Yes. Bring a photo of the space, or say how much sun it has, and someone on the lot will lay out a few plants that will live there.

My plant is dying. Can you help?

Usually. Bring a photo, or the plant. The staff will ask about sun, watering, ants and whether water is standing in the hole, which covers most of it.

Can Amanda's crew plant what I buy?

Yes. Buy the plants and take them home, or have Amanda's crew plant them for you.

Do you sell to landscapers?

Yes. Landscapers and contractors from as far as Little Rock buy plants and supplies here.

Can you order something you don't have?

Usually. If it isn't on the lot, Amanda will try to find it.

What are your hours?

Hours. The garden center is at 7901 Dollarway Road, White Hall.
