Most yards don't look like the people who live there

Arkansas summers are long and hot, and clay soil holds water through every spring storm. When a yard is planted without a plan:

  • Plants end up in the wrong spot

    Whatever is blooming goes wherever there's a gap. A shade plant lands in full sun, shrubs crowd the windows within a few years, and by August half the bed is struggling.

  • Homeowners give up on the yard

    After paying to plant the same bed twice, most people stop trying and stop looking at it.

  • A yard should feel like home

    A yard is supposed to look like the people who live there, and that takes a plan drawn for that one yard.

Meet Amanda, the landscape designer who measures every yard herself

Amanda designs every landscaping project at Amanda's Villa. She walks each yard in person, notes the sun, the shade and the soil, and draws a planting plan for that yard alone.

Homeowners bring her a magazine page, a neighbor's yard or a photo on their phone, and she works out how to make that look grow here. When something in the picture won't survive an Arkansas summer, she says so plainly and suggests what will.

Every plant in her plans comes from the Amanda's Villa garden center in White Hall, one of the widest selections of shrubs and trees in the state.

A landscaping company serving homes all over Arkansas

Landscaping in Arkansas means planting for long, hot summers and heavy clay soil. Amanda's Villa handles landscape design and landscape installation for homes all over the state of Arkansas, and Amanda measures and draws every plan herself.

The crew plants flower beds, foundation shrubs and shade gardens, edges every bed and finishes it with mulch or river rock from the garden center. When a yard needs a patio, walkway or retaining wall, the hardscape teams build that part, so the stonework and the planting are planned together from the start.

Landscape design questions

Does Amanda really come out herself?

Yes. Amanda measures and designs every landscaping project personally.

What if I already know what I want?

Bring the picture. Amanda will work out how to make it grow here, and she will tell you if anything in it won't survive an Arkansas summer.

Do you plant shade gardens?

Yes. Amanda plans for full sun, part shade and full shade, and every plant is chosen for the light its spot actually gets.

Which shrubs work best for the front of a house?

It depends on how much sun the front of the house gets and how high the windows sit. Amanda chooses shrubs that suit the light and stay in scale with the house, and the garden center usually carries three or four varieties of each to choose between.

Can I plant it myself?

Yes. Amanda can hand over the plan and the plants for anyone who would rather do the digging.

What is the difference between landscaping and hardscaping?

Landscaping is the living part of a yard: plants, flower beds, edging and mulch. Hardscaping is the built part: patios, walkways and retaining walls. Amanda's Villa does both, so the planting and the stonework can be designed together.

Do you landscape sloped or hillside yards?

Yes. On a hillside, the hardscape teams can build retaining walls to level the ground, and Amanda plants the beds they create.

How much does landscaping cost?

Every project is a custom quote. Amanda measures the yard and designs the plan first, then quotes the job before anything goes in the ground.

Are the project photos your own work?

Yes. Every project photo on this page shows a yard landscaped by Amanda's Villa.

How far do you travel?

Amanda's Villa works all over the state of Arkansas.
