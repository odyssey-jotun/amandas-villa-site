Block walls, steps and a paver walk across a farmhouse front

The front of a black farmhouse sat a step above open lawn. If you live in Woodlawn, we commit to crafting you a yard you're proud to come home to

Woodlawn sits in Cleveland County, which the Saline River crosses from near its northwest corner to near its southeast corner.3 Under nearly eight of every ten acres within a mile of Woodlawn, USDA soil maps show a seasonal water table within about two feet of the surface, and nearly one acre in five is floodplain that floods often.2 Arkansas averages about 50 inches of rain a year.1

So your project starts with a free estimate and a transit survey of how your ground falls, and the drainage is planned before the stone and the plants.

A gravel base under every walk

Your walk or patio sits on 8 to 10 inches of compacted gravel with a finer base on top, so the stone stays level through wet springs and dry summers.

Drainage goes in first

Downspout lines and French drains go in before the stone and the plants, so roof water and runoff have somewhere to go.

Woodlawn landscaping and hardscaping questions

Has Amanda's Villa done work in Woodlawn?

Yes. The curved bed and flagstone walk and the farmhouse walls, steps and walk on this page are both at Woodlawn homes, and the teams work all over the state of Arkansas.

What does Woodlawn's soil mean for a walk, patio or flower bed?

Under nearly eight of every ten acres within a mile of Woodlawn, USDA soil maps show a seasonal water table within about two feet of the surface.2 So every walk and patio sits on a compacted gravel base, and drainage is planned before anything is planted.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the project. At the farmhouse on this page, the first wall was going up in late April, and the finished photos were taken in mid-May. Once work begins, the crew stays on your project until it is finished, and weather is the only common delay.

Is the estimate free?

Yes. A member of the hardscape team visits the property, surveys it with a transit and provides an estimate at no cost. Every project is a custom quote.
